Annex · Methodology & sources

Every number, on the record

Self-reported crowd surveys skew toward engaged, higher-earning, urban respondents. Company-sourced benchmarks are cleaner but narrower. This page never mixes bases inside one comparison without saying so.

Net ⇄ gross conversion ratios (and why they're approximations)

Ratios are derived from each country's national average wage statistics (or statutory rates where noted) and applied uniformly. Real IT salaries sit above the average, and progressive systems tax them harder — so converted gross figures for high earners are, if anything, understated. That's precisely the argument for demanding basis labels on any benchmark.

Known limitations
  • Serbia's FishingBooker sample skews toward product / foreign-client companies → reads high vs. the whole market.
  • Slovenia's survey publishes no seniority split; its all-level median is compared against other countries' senior tiers only where labeled.
  • Bulgaria, BiH, Montenegro and Albania lack a matched IT-vs-national-average pair inside one methodology — shown as explicit data gaps.
  • The salary translator applies average-wage ratios uniformly; contribution caps, allowances and progressive brackets are not modeled.
  • Export bases differ (BoP services vs. industry revenue). Never rank them literally.
  • Flags are simplified inline-SVG marks — deliberately, so they render identically on every OS (emoji flags don't load on Windows).
  • Snapshot: public sources as of August 2026. Corrections and fresher data → open a PR or issue.
